Planner capture: privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Planner capture is a personal tool built and used only by Josh Nanda. It turns his own voice notes into items in his private Notion workspace and events on his own Google Calendar.

It asks Google for one permission, to create and edit calendar events. It uses that permission only to add, move, and set reminders on the events Josh asks for. It doesn't read, sell, share, or keep any other Google data, and no one else's data is collected.

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party connections. Questions: nanda.joshjm@gmail.com.
